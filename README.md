    <!-- Surah 1: Al-Fatiha -->
    <div class="surah">
        <h2>Surah Al-Fatiha (الفاتحة)</h2>
        <div class="arabic">
            بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ<br>
            الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ<br>
            الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ<br>
            مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ<br>
            إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ<br>
            اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ<br>
            صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ<br>
            غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
            <strong>English:</strong> In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. All praise is for Allah, Lord of the worlds. The Most Gracious, the Most Merciful. Master of the Day of Judgment. You alone we worship and You alone we ask for help. Guide us on the Straight Path, the path of those You have blessed, not of those who have earned Your anger nor of those who go astray.<br>
            <strong>Roman Urdu:</strong> Bismillah hir-Rahman nir-Raheem. Sab tareef Allah ke liye hai, jo sab jahanon ka Rubb hai. Bohat Meherban, Bohat Reham wala. Din e Aakhir ka Malik. Hum sirf aap ki ibadat karte hain aur sirf aap se madad mangte hain. Humein seedha raasta dikhaen, unka raasta jinhon ne aap ki inayat pai, na ki unka jinhon ne aap ka gussa hasil kiya ya jo gumrah hain.
        </div>
    </div>

    <!-- Surah 2: Al-Ikhlas -->
    <div class="surah">
        <h2>Surah Al-Ikhlas (الإخلاص)</h2>
        <div class="arabic">
            بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ<br>
            قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ<br>
            اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ<br>
            لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ<br>
            وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
            <strong>English:</strong> Say, He is Allah, the One. Allah, the Eternal Refuge. He neither begets nor is born, nor is there to Him any equivalent.<br>
            <strong>Roman Urdu:</strong> Kaho, Allah ek hai. Allah, hamesha ke liye sahara. Na usne janam diya, na use janam diya gaya, aur koi uska humsar nahi.
        </div>
    </div>

    <hr>

    <!-- Surah 3: Al-Falaq -->
    <div class="surah">
        <h2>Surah Al-Falaq (الفلق)</h2>
        <div class="arabic">
            بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ<br>
            قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ الْفَلَقِ<br>
            مِن شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ<br>
            وَمِن شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ<br>
            وَمِن شَرِّ النَّفَّاثَاتِ فِي الْعُقَدِ<br>
            وَمِن شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
            <strong>English:</strong> Say, I seek refuge with the Lord of the daybreak, from the evil of what He has created, from the evil of darkness when it settles, from the evil of those who practice witchcraft, and from the evil of the envier when he envies.<br>
            <strong>Roman Urdu:</strong> Kaho, main din ke Rubb ke paas panah mangta hoon, us cheez ke sharr se jo usne banai, andhere ke sharr se jab wo chha jaye, jin logon ke jaado ka sharr, aur jalan karne wale ka sharr jab wo jalan kare.
        </div>
    </div>

    <hr>

    <!-- Surah 4: An-Nas -->
    <div class="surah">
        <h2>Surah An-Nas (الناس)</h2>
        <div class="arabic">
            بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ<br>
            قُلْ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ النَّاسِ<br>
            مَلِكِ النَّاسِ<br>
            إِلَٰهِ النَّاسِ<br>
            مِن شَرِّ الْوَسْوَاسِ الْخَنَّاسِ<br>
            الَّذِي يُوَسْوِسُ فِي صُدُورِ النَّاسِ<br>
            مِنَ الْجِنَّةِ وَالنَّاسِ
        </div>
        <div class="translation">
            <strong>English:</strong> Say, I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind, the King of mankind, the God of mankind, from the evil whisperer who withdraws, who whispers in the hearts of mankind, from among jinn and mankind.<br>
            <strong>Roman Urdu:</strong> Kaho, main insano ke Rubb ke paas panah mangta hoon, insano ka Malik, insano ka Khuda, un sharr se jo chupke se khayal dalte hain, jo insano ke dilon me khayal dalte hain, jin aur insano me se.
        </div>
    </div>
